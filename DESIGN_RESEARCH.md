# Northstar Loop design research

Northstar Loop's product UI was shaped after reviewing contemporary productivity, planning, task-management, dashboard, onboarding, and focus-work examples on Dribbble before the main UI implementation.

The recurring patterns worth carrying forward were:

- one dominant action or priority above secondary analytics;
- modular cards with a single informational job each;
- generous whitespace and strong typography instead of decorative chrome;
- calm, compact status treatments rather than saturated dashboards;
- progressive disclosure for planning and editing;
- sparse focus experiences that remove navigation noise;
- clear empty, loading, error, and at-risk states;
- mobile layouts that reflow actions rather than merely shrinking desktop UI.

Northstar Loop intentionally does not reproduce a reference composition, illustration, logo, icon system, or branded asset. Its visual system is original: black and white are dominant, neutral grays establish hierarchy, semantic color is restrained, Inter carries typography, borders and spacing create structure, and the most important interface element is always the next useful action.
